This is the useful surprise in the negative result: declared loss of the transcript did not, in this trajectory, induce an external representation. ## Interpretation

This is one exploratory forensic compaction treatment, not evidence that declared compaction generally prevents or induces persistence. The trace establishes the absence of a model-created filesystem artifact in this run and the observed action sequence, but it cannot establish a causal performance benefit or behavior under uninterrupted full context. A later matched continuous-context condition and predeclared repetitions are required for those claims.

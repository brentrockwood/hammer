Does making reopenable append visible and available change the H0 trajectory under declared transcript loss?

## Forensic reading

The append mode was listed and enabled, but the model never requested it. It made no write attempt and left no model-created filesystem state. Its 276 accepted actions were 139 opens and 137 reads; unlike the matched control, it issued no closes. Both declared full-reset boundaries occurred, and the final snapshot is identical to the fixture.

The paired control, under the same fixture and sampling seed, made 130 opens, 127 reads, 20 closes, and two rejected actions. The append-visible condition therefore changed the observed action trajectory without producing append uptake, an output file, or a reusable artifact. This one pair cannot attribute the difference to append semantics rather than the additional grammar line and its downstream prompt effects, and it does not support a claim about the general usefulness of append.

Does explicit position within the declared compaction schedule change the external state the model creates and consults while solving one closed graph-routing task?

The host discarded the model transcript after turns 70 and 140 while preserving /work, labeled each checkpoint and, in the horizon condition, stated the total and remaining turn budget. ## Forensic reading

Both injected continuation messages identified the checkpoint number and absolute turn: first `Checkpoint 1 of 2` after turn 70, then `Checkpoint 2 of 2` after turn 140. The model made no write attempt. Its 278 accepted actions were two directory enumerations, 140 opens, and 136 reads; the final snapshot is identical to the initial fixture.

The label resolved the temporal ambiguity in the original C48 continuation, but it did not change the observed external-state behavior in this one run. The model found the terminal before the first boundary and again after the second, then continued reading until the cap. This does not isolate why it kept scanning or predict what the declared-horizon condition will do.
